## Complexity one-liners (spoken)

- Two pointers over a string/array: O(n) time, O(1) space.
- Counting into a Map: O(n) time, O(k) space (k = distinct keys).
- Sorting: O(n log n), the answer to "can you do better?" is usually a HashMap.
- Nested loop over the same array: O(n²) — say it out loud when you write one.
