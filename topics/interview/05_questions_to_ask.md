# 05 — Questions to Ask

Ask 3–5. Choose based on the conversation; do not read them like a list.

---

## About the role

1. What does success look like in the first 6–12 months?
2. What's the biggest quality pain the team has today?
3. How is testing owned — central team, embedded QE, or developers?
4. What does the release process look like today?
5. What's the balance between framework work and hands-on testing?

## About the system

6. What's the architecture — monolith, services, event-driven?
7. What are the highest-risk flows?
8. How do services communicate — REST, GraphQL, gRPC, events?
9. How is test data managed across services?
10. How are environments provisioned — shared, ephemeral, cloud?

## About quality

11. How do you measure quality today — escaped defects, flaky rate, coverage?
12. What's the flaky-test situation?
13. How do you decide what to automate vs test manually?
14. How does testing fit into CI/CD and release validation?
15. What's the biggest source of production incidents?

## About the team

16. How does the team collaborate with developers and product?
17. How do you handle disagreements about test strategy?
18. What does onboarding look like?
19. What's the on-call/support model?
20. What are you most excited about in the next year?

## About growth

21. What skills do you want this person to grow?
22. What's the career path for SDETs here?
23. How do you support learning and tool adoption?

---

## Questions that signal seniority

- "How do you decide what the framework owns versus what product teams own?"
- "How do you measure whether the test suite is trustworthy?"
- "Where does E2E currently hurt, and what would you push down?"
- "How do you handle contract testing between services?"
- "What's the release gate today, and what would you change?"
- "How do you balance speed and coverage on a small team?"

---

## Avoid

- Questions answered on the website.
- Questions that imply you haven't read the role.
- "What does the company do?"
- More than 5 questions.
- Questions about salary/benefits in the first technical round (unless appropriate).
