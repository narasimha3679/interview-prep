# Interview Preparation — Study Pack

Company-agnostic interview preparation: behavioral stories, project narratives, system design for
testers, and mock questions.

**Last verified:** November 2026.

---

## 60-second cram

1. **Structure every answer.** What → why → how → trade-off → result. Never a one-liner.
2. **Use STAR for behaviorals.** Situation, Task, Action, Result. Keep it under 90 seconds.
3. **Own your projects.** Lead with engineering decisions, not tools.
4. **Name the trade-off.** Senior engineers talk about what they chose *not* to do.
5. **Be honest about gaps.** "I haven't done X; here's how I'd reason about it" beats bluffing.
6. **Ask good questions.** They signal seniority and genuine interest.
7. **Prepare stories, not scripts.** Rehearse the shape, not the exact words.
8. **Close with impact.** Metrics, adoption, reliability, speed, or risk reduction.

**The one line that shows depth:**

> "I optimize for the fastest reliable signal for the risk being changed — not for the largest
> number of tests."

## How to use this pack

- Build a **story bank** of 6–8 real situations.
- Practice each out loud, timed.
- Record yourself; cut filler and jargon.
- For each project, prepare a 2-minute pitch and 5 likely follow-ups.
- Do one full mock interview before the real one.
